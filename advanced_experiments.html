Chunked Prefill Analysis:
  • First request at 256K: 54.8s (cold start)

Cold Start Issue: The first request at 256K took 54.8s, likely due to JIT compilation or cache initialization. Subsequent requests are 15x faster (3.6s). In production, keep-alive connections or warmup requests can mitigate this.
