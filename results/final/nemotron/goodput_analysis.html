What is Goodput?

Definition (DistServe paper): Goodput = throughput of requests that meet the SLO. Raw throughput includes all requests (good + bad). The gap between raw and goodput is "wasted" work — requests that completed too late to be useful.

The Goodput Wall

Visualizing the cliff: As concurrency increases, raw throughput grows but goodput stays flat or drops. The "wall" is where goodput stops growing — beyond this point, adding more concurrency just creates more bad requests.

Takeaway: Raw throughput grows 5.7x from c1 to c256 (94 to 540 tok/s). But 2s goodput only grows 1.0x (54 to 54 tok/s). The GPU is working harder but not producing more useful output. This is why goodput matters for capacity planning.

Why goodput changes everything: With raw throughput, we estimated 370 GPUs (memory-limited). With goodput, the estimate depends on SLO:
  • 2s SLO: ~526 GPUs (much more than raw estimate)
  • 4s SLO: ~228 GPUs
  • 8s SLO: ~119 GPUs
The SLO definition is the single most important variable in capacity planning.
