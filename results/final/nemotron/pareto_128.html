5. Analysis

Mamba Advantage: Constant-Time Decode

Nemotron's Mamba layers process tokens in constant time regardless of sequence length. This is why decode speed at c1 (112 tok/s) is 5x faster than Qwen NVFP4 (22 tok/s). The 29 Mamba layers avoid the O(n) attention cost that limits Qwen's 40 MoE layers.

Scaling Behavior

Throughput scales 7x from c1 to c64 (0.75 → 5.33 req/s). Beyond c64, throughput plateaus — the GPU is fully saturated. The scaling exponent is 0.536 (sublinear), meaning each doubling of concurrency yields ~45% more throughput.

Latency Trade-off

At c64, E2E latency reaches 6.9s (vs 1.3s at c1). ITL grows from 9ms to 50ms. This is acceptable for batch workloads but too slow for interactive chat. The sweet spot for interactive use is c1-c4.

Power Efficiency

Peak efficiency: 19.24 tok/s/W at c64 (reasoning). Power consumption is 31-41W across all concurrency levels — a narrow range. The GPU power floor is ~31W even at c1, meaning idle GPU still consumes significant power. Batching at c64 amortizes this idle power across 64 requests.
