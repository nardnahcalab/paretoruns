B4: Queue Depth & Rejection Rate

Result: ZERO FAILURES at all concurrency levels tested (c1-c512, 128-4096 tok output). vLLM's continuous batching handles extreme load without rejecting requests.

Why zero failures? vLLM uses continuous batching — requests are processed in a queue, not all at once. High concurrency increases latency (queuing) but doesn't cause failures. The GPU processes requests as fast as it can, and the rest wait in the queue. This is fundamentally different from batch-processing systems that reject when full.
