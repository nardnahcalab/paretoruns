4. Key Insights

Mamba vs Attention: 5x Gap

Nemotron's Mamba layers process tokens in O(1) time, while Qwen's attention layers are O(n). At c1, Nemotron achieves 112 tok/s vs Qwen's 23 tok/s — a 4.9x advantage. This gap widens at higher concurrency because Mamba scales better with batch size.

Dense vs MoE: Muse is 16x Slower

Muse activates all 30B params per token. Nemotron activates only 3B (10x fewer). At c1, Muse achieves 8 tok/s vs Nemotron's 112 tok/s — a 14x gap. Dense models are fundamentally slower on GB10 because they can't exploit conditional compute.

FP8 vs NVFP4: Negligible Difference

Qwen FP8 and NVFP4 show nearly identical performance (120 vs 147 tok/s peak). The 4-bit quantization provides 2x memory savings with only 18% speed difference. NVFP4 is the better choice for memory-constrained deployments.

Efficiency Winner: Nemotron

At peak efficiency, Nemotron achieves 19.2 tok/s/W — 4.4x better than Qwen NVFP4 (4.3 tok/s/W). For cost-sensitive deployments, Nemotron is the clear winner on GB10.
